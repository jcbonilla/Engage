How to interpret the comparison

Percentage difference = episode downloads divided by the unrounded Q3 show average, minus one. Above average means strictly greater than that average. This reference includes the episodes being evaluated. It is a retrospective comparison, not a rolling baseline or independent target.

One low episode can lower the mean and increase the share above it. CMO has six of seven above its mean, partly because its recent September 30 release has 135 downloads. This is not listener retention, a quality score, or a causal content effect.

Use first-30-day downloads for more comparable outcomes. In Q4, measure against the fixed Q3 baseline while reviewing publication ages and formats.
